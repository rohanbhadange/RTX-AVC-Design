# CAD sources and ownership

This archive contains project CAD and manufacturer/supplier reference geometry. Third-party ownership and any original terms remain with the respective owners. Publication here does not grant a new license or relicense vendor assets. No blanket software or CAD license is asserted for this mixed-source archive.

Native filenames, source directory relationships and CAD bytes are preserved. Imported native assemblies may contain manufacturer component names or historical source-path fragments. Some project representations are envelopes, simplified extractions or standard-hardware approximations; their presence does not establish supplier approval or as-built accuracy.

Identifiable source families include Holybro flight-controller/compute/power hardware, Intel RealSense D455, NVIDIA/Seeed compute geometry, T-Motor propulsion references, Pololu regulators, Basicmicro RoboClaw, Lyn-Tron spacers and standard mounting hardware. Individual component rights remain with their respective owners, including subcomponents in imported supplier assemblies.
